  scripts/build_render_pilot_dataset.py (resized 1280px pilot dataset),
  scripts/evaluate_pilot.py (writes PSNR/SSIM from artifacts).

4. WHICH HYPOTHESES FAILED / WHAT IS UNKNOWN

- The deterministic candidate is 13x smaller (SPZ) and ~19x faster to build than
  B0, at 10x fewer splats.  This is a cost result, not a quality result.
- Its image quality could not be ranked: the trainer (Brush) renders eval views
  only during its own training loop, and writing a second rasterizer was out of
  scope, so there is no common renderer to produce candidate held-out images.
  P/U/R therefore stay null, not zero.
- The candidate's peak RSS (2.65 GiB) is confounded by loading the full source
  mesh and full-resolution photos, unlike B0's 40-image 1280px input.
- No claim that any result is photorealistic or a measurement-accuracy gain.
